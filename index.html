Timber Expertise

Wood & Material Selection Guide

Understand the durability, grain characteristics, and ideal applications for the premium timbers and engineered boards we work with.

Burmese / CP Teak (Sagwan)

Grade A Solid

The gold standard in Indian joinery. Rich in natural oils, making it virtually impervious to termites, rot, and seasonal warping.

Termite Resistance
Moisture Endurance
Expected Lifespan 50+ Years
Best For: Main Doors, Dining Tables, Heirloom Beds

Indian Sheesham (Rosewood)

Solid Hardwood

Celebrated for its dramatic dark and golden grain patterns. High tensile strength and density make it ideal for carved furniture.

Termite Resistance
Moisture Endurance
Expected Lifespan 35+ Years
Best For: Dining Sets, Coffee Tables, Temple (Mandir) Units

BWP Marine Ply (IS:710)

Engineered Core

Bonded with unextended phenol formaldehyde synthetic resin. Withstands 72 hours of continuous boiling water immersion without delamination.

Termite Resistance
Moisture Endurance
Expected Lifespan 25+ Years
Best For: Modular Kitchens, Vanity Counters, Utility Cabinets

Natural Oak & Walnut Veneer

Luxury Surface

Genuine thin-sliced hardwood flitches pressed on calibrated marine plywood, coated with anti-yellowing PU polish.

Visual Luxury
Scratch Resistance
Expected Lifespan 20+ Years
Best For: Fluted Wall Panels, Master Wardrobe Fronts, TV Consoles
Precision Workflow

How We Bring Your Vision To Life

A transparent 4-stage process designed to ensure exact millimeter fitment, zero surprises, and pristine on-site execution.

01

Free Site Measurement

Our master carpenter visits your property with laser measurement tools and timber samples to evaluate your space and moisture conditions.

02

3D Design & Specs

We provide precise 2D/3D layout elevations, hardware specifications (Blum/Ebco), and a transparent itemized quotation.

03

Workshop Fabrication

Wood cutting, mortise joinery, edge-banding, and primary polish are completed in our dust-controlled workshop to keep your home clean.

04

Dust-Free Fitting

Modular components are assembled on-site with soft-close calibration, final touch-up polish, and thorough post-installation cleanup.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about carpentry rates, site visits, warranties, and woodwork care.

Solid wood (like Teak and Sheesham) is natural timber carved directly from tree trunks, offering timeless aesthetics, heirloom durability (50+ years), and refinishability. Marine BWP 710 Plywood is an engineered wood board treated with phenol resin that resists boiling water and moisture, making it the superior technical choice for kitchen carcasses and wardrobes.
Yes, all our custom woodwork comes with an official 5-Year Craftsmanship Guarantee covering joinery structural integrity, hinge alignment, and delamination. Furthermore, premium branded hardware (like Blum and Häfele) includes up to 10 years of manufacturer warranty.
We fabricate up to 85% of all components (cutting, edge-banding, initial sanding, and polishing) in our private workshop. Only final modular assembly and soft-close calibration occur at your residence, cutting on-site dust and noise by more than 80%.
We operate on a transparent milestone basis: 40% initial deposit upon 3D design approval to purchase raw timber and plywood, 40% upon completion of workshop modular carcass fabrication (which you can inspect in-person or via video call), and the final 20% only after complete on-site installation and your 100% sign-off.
